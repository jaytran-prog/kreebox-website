Overview

TSC.ai builds intelligence platforms for some of the world's largest organizations — Fortune 500 companies and NGOs operating across 95 countries. When I joined, there was no product design function, no design system, and no team.

As the design lead, my focus was the UX/UI: translating product requirements into user-tested interfaces, building the design system from scratch, and maintaining design quality from early concepts through to production. I worked alongside PM and Engineering throughout.
